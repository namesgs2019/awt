Conventional Process vs AWT Proposed Process

Challenges of Conventional Phosphorus Removal

Chemical phosphorus removal requires alum or PAC dosing, which can increase sludge generation and leave residual ions such as sulfate and chloride in the treatment system.

01

Activated Sludge Process

The conventional activated sludge process removes organic pollutants, but additional chemical treatment is often required for phosphorus control and reuse-grade water quality.

Chemical Phosphorus Removal
