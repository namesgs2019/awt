ECM Technology

Electro-coagulation & Ceramic Membrane

ECM is AWT's core water purification technology that integrates electro-coagulation with ceramic membrane filtration. It differs from chemical coagulation and PFAS-based polymeric membranes by reducing residual sulfate and chloride ion issues, improving sludge handling, and using durable ceramic membranes with clean-water permeability of 2,000-3,000 L/m2.hr.bar under 100nm pore conditions.

Electro-coagulation & Ceramic Membrane Technology

AWT's ECM (Electro-coagulation & Ceramic Membrane) technology is a core water purification platform designed to reduce chemical dependency, overcome the limitations of disposable plastic membrane systems, and deliver efficient, scalable, and sustainable water treatment performance.

ECM integrates electro-coagulation, which aggregates pollutants without conventional chemical dosing, with ceramic membrane filtration, which provides high permeability, strong durability, and efficient backwashing. Together, these technologies create an optimized process for drinking water, sewage treatment, wastewater treatment, industrial effluent treatment, water reuse, and desalination pretreatment.

The Power of Electro-coagulation

Electro-coagulation continuously and efficiently aggregates pollutants within minutes. It also supports organic matter treatment and phosphorus removal through electro-oxidation.

Unlike chemical coagulation, electro-coagulation does not leave excessive residual sulfate or chloride ions that can burden downstream reverse osmosis systems. It also reduces membrane fouling caused by chemical residues and helps sludge detach more easily during backwashing.

Innovative Ceramic Membranes

Ceramic membranes have long been recognized for their excellent physical properties, but cost was once a barrier to broader adoption. Through innovation in material processing and module design, AWT has developed ceramic membrane technologies that are more practical for scalable water treatment applications.

Compared with polymeric membranes, ceramic membranes offer superior durability, environmental compatibility, high permeability, and efficient backwashing. Even at a 100 nm filtration pore size, AWT's ceramic membrane technology can achieve clean-water permeability in the range of 2,000-3,000 L/m2.hr.bar, supporting high operational efficiency.

No Chemicals. No Plastics.

Conventional water and wastewater treatment systems often require large land areas, chemical additives, disposable plastic filters, and energy-intensive processes. These limitations can lead to secondary pollution, increased sludge generation, fouling issues, and reduced water reuse efficiency.

AWT aims to provide a cleaner and more efficient alternative by combining electro-coagulation and ceramic membrane filtration into a compact and future-ready ECM process.

Technology Principle

Step 1. Pollutants

Various ions, colloids, particles and contaminants are present in raw water or wastewater.

Step 2. Agglomeration

Electro-coagulation aggregates pollutants within minutes without chemical coagulants.

Step 3. Separation

Ceramic membrane filtration separates agglomerated particles and suspended solids.

Step 4. Optimized Reuse

The treated water becomes suitable for reuse, RO feedwater, or further purification.
