Advanced Water Technology

Advanced Water Technology for the Future of Water

AWT provides purification technologies for the water environment, from materials, components, equipment, and process design to drinking water, sewage treatment, wastewater treatment, water reuse, desalination, and portable facilities. Based on ECM technology, AWT minimizes chemicals, plastics, facility footprint, sludge discharge, and energy consumption while pursuing AI-based autonomous operation.

Core Message

No Chemicals. No Plastics. Smarter Water Treatment.

Conventional systems often rely on chemical additives, large-scale facilities, polymeric membranes, and energy-intensive processes. AWT provides an alternative approach through electro-coagulation and ceramic membrane filtration.

Chemical-Free Coagulation

Electro-coagulation aggregates pollutants without conventional chemical dosing.

Ceramic Membrane Filtration

Durable ceramic membranes provide high permeability and efficient backwashing.

Water Reuse Optimization

ECM improves feedwater quality for RO systems and helps reduce brine generation.

AI-Based Autonomous Operation

AWT aims to maximize operational efficiency through AI-based autonomous control.

Solutions

Integrated Solutions Across the Water Cycle

Water Reuse & Phosphorus Removal

Chemical-free phosphorus removal and RO-optimized water reuse using electro-coagulation and ceramic membrane filtration.

  • No residual sulfate or chloride ions
  • Minimum sludge discharge
  • Reduced RO fouling burden
  • Higher water reuse efficiency

Tap Water Purification

Reliable drinking water treatment from reservoir intake to water tower supply.

  • Safe water supply
  • Activated carbon polishing
  • Disinfection-ready process

Sewage Treatment

Compact sewage treatment for organic matter, nitrogen, phosphorus, suspended solids, and E. coli removal.

  • Organic removal
  • Nitrogen and phosphorus removal
  • Suspended solid removal
  • Optional water reuse through RO

Industrial Wastewater Treatment & Reuse

Integrated process combining flotation, advanced oxidation, ECM, RO, activated carbon, and sludge dehydration.

  • Industrial effluent treatment
  • Water reuse
  • Reduced discharge burden

Desalination & Brine Treatment

ECM-based pretreatment and ceramic membrane systems for desalination and brine reduction applications.

  • RO feedwater optimization
  • Reduced fouling burden
  • Harsh water conditions

Portable Water Treatment Facility

A modular 40ft containerized plant capable of supplying more than 400 tons of potable water per day.

  • Easy transportation and installation
  • Solar power integration available
  • RO and zeolite filtration can be added
